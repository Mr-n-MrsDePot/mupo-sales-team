# MUPO Entertainment (MUPO TV)

**Founder:** Michele Mupo  
**Brand:** MUPO TV  
**Focus:** Entertainment media connecting brands, artists, and audiences through television, digital content, magazine publishing, and artist development.

## Positioning

MUPO TV is an entertainment platform that helps:
1. **Brands** reach engaged audiences via sponsorships, commercial spots, and magazine placements.
2. **Creators & hosts** launch their own shows through TV membership packages.
3. **Artists** accelerate careers with structured development packages.

## What We Sell (Product Lines)

| Product | Typical Range | Ideal Buyer |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| TV Advertising & Sponsorship | $10,000 – $80,000 | Brand CMOs, agency media buyers, founders of consumer brands |
| 30-Second Commercial Spots | $2,500 – $15,000 | Local/regional advertisers, DTC brands testing TV |
| TV Membership (own show) | $1,000 – $5,000 | Coaches, experts, entertainers, entrepreneurs with an audience |
| Magazine Advertising | $500 – $8,000 | Lifestyle brands, beauty, fashion, hospitality |
| Artist Development | $2,000 – $25,000 | Musicians, performers, emerging talent |

## Sales Philosophy

- High-ticket, relationship-driven sales — not spam volume.
- Agents research, personalize, qualify, and prepare; humans close deals ≥ $5k and all complex sponsorships.
- Truth over hype: never invent metrics. Use only verified figures from the knowledge base.
- Commission-oriented culture: every action is logged for attribution.

## Brand Voice

- Professional but warm
- Entertainment-industry confident (not corporate stiff)
- Clear value, no jargon walls
- Respectful of prospect time
